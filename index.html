About me

I've been doing AI data work since 2024, mostly on Remotasks, Appen, CrowdGen and Outlier. A lot of it is first-person video: watching what the hands are doing and writing it down exactly the way the project rules say.

I also work in RLHF: evaluating AI responses and explaining my judgments in writing so the model can improve.

The part I'm best at is spotting the small stuff. A wrong verb, a miscounted object, a segment that should have been split. I also write short notes explaining what is wrong and which rule it breaks.

I studied English and Literature at The Presbyterian University, and I'm fluent in English and Swahili. I work remotely and don't need much supervision.
